# Shatter Run - thumbnail A/B variants (1920x1080, Blender renders of the real game parts)

- `A_new_balls.png` - "NEW BALLS!": Supernova + Black Hole huge in the blocky first-person hands. Tests: does showing new items/skins win?
- `B_reach_zone10.png` - "CAN YOU REACH ZONE 10?": corridor through all 10 zone colours to a ZONE 10 sign. Tests: challenge / goal framing.
- `C_mega_ball.png` - "MEGA BALL!": giant ball ploughing through a glass wall, crates and vases flying. Tests: pure destruction / power fantasy.
- `D_10_zones.png` - "10 ZONES!": 5-panel collage of Frost, Magma, Aquarium, Sky, Core. Tests: variety / amount of content.
- `E_minimal.png` - one clean perfect shatter + logo only. Tests: does less text win?

How to test (Roblox calls it "thumbnail personalization"; menu names may differ slightly):
1. create.roblox.com -> Creations -> Shatter Run -> the experience's Home Page / Thumbnails settings.
2. Upload these 5 images (each must be under 3 MB and at most 1920x1080), tick **Active** on 2-5 of them, save and start.
3. Roblox shows each one to random players, measures the qualified play-through rate (clicks that turn into real plays), then shows the winner more. Let it run 1-2 weeks, keep the winner, and make the next round of variants in its style.
